# Character Progression Brainstorm

This document explores potential methodologies for character growth, aiming to balance narrative-driven rewards with player-controlled advancement.

## 1. Core Principles
- **Infinite Growth**: Attributes can reach 20 and potentially exceed it through specialized milestones or mastery.
- **Dual Tracks**: Advancement comes from both Narrative (GM-awarded) and Mechanical (Player-spent) sources.
- **Card Compatibility**: Progression should naturally lead to new Traits, Abilities, and Items in card format.

---

## 2. Methodology Proposals

### Proposal A: Milestone-Based Development (Hybrid)
This system rewards "Significant Narrative Moments" with **Development Points (DP)**.

- **Narrative Rewards**: The GM awards specific Qualities or Talents directly based on the story (e.g., "Slayer of the Ice Wyrm" quality).
- **Player Spending**: Players receive 1 DP per milestone to invest in their "Mechanical Core":
    - **Attribute +1**: Costs 1 DP (up to 20).
    - **New Trait/Ability**: Costs DP equal to its Numerical Power Level.
    - **Upgrade Rank**: Pay the difference between current and new Numerical Level.

### Proposal B: The Progression Deck (Card-Focused)
Leveraging the physical reference cards, characters manage a "Hand of Capability."

- **The Hand Limit**: A character’s Numerical Level (1-10+) determines their **Capability Budget**.
- **Advancement**: Upon "leveling up" (via XP or Sessions), players can draft new cards from a pool (Novice to Master).
- **Swapping**: Players can swap cards during long rests, but their total "Numerical Level" of equipped cards cannot exceed their Budget.
- **Attribute Growth**: Attributes increase at flat levels (e.g., every 2nd level).

### Proposal C: Point-Buy Pathways (Granular)
A more traditional "Experience Point" system where every action contributes.

- **Growth Points (GP)**: Awarded at the end of each session.
- **Pathways**: Players invest GP into specific "Paths" (e.g., *Path of the Vanguard*, *Path of the Scholar*).
- **Unlocks**: Reaching certain GP thresholds in a Path unlocks pre-defined Abilities/Traits and Attribute clusters.
- **Over-Capping**: Deep investment in a single Path (Mastery) allows attributes to break the 20 cap.

---

## 3. Power Level Mapping
To improve balancing, we can translate the existing narrative **Categories** into defined **Numerical Power Levels**.

| Category | Numerical Level | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Novice** | 1 | Basic techniques, common traits. |
| **Adept** | 2-3 | Specialized training, uncommon expertise. |
| **Master** | 4-5 | Peak competence, rare powers. |
| **Grandmaster** | 6+ | Legendary feats, reality-bending capabilities. |

---

## 4. Attribute Growth Beyond 20
Characters have a natural "Mortal Limit" of 20. Breaking this limit requires **Legendary Growth**:

- **The Cost of Mastery**: To increase an attribute from 20 to 21 costs double (or requires a specific Grandmaster Trait).
- **Scaling Returns**: Beyond 20, the mathematical benefit shifts from adding dice to granting **Static Multipliers** or **Automatic Successes** on specific outcomes.
