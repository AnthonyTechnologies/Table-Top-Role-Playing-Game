# Workshop Proposal: Invoking Quality Tags (Revised for 1-20 Attributes)

With an **Attribute range of 1 to 20**, the mechanical impact of a Quality must be significantly higher to satisfy the goal of accounting for "more than half the value" compared to an Attribute. 

## Revised Baseline
- **Attribute Range:** 1 to 20 (Typical).
- **Max Attribute Impact:** +20.
- **Target Quality Impact:** > +10 (at high levels).

---

## Stability Analysis: The "Mathematical Ceiling"
The USER correctly identified a core risk: **Doubling the Attribute** (Proposal 1) at upper bounds (15-20) leads to extreme mathematical instability. 

- **The Problem:** If a character with an Attribute of 20 doubles their bonus to +40, they effectively supersede the entire resolution scale (Trivial 10 to Grandmaster 35). This removes the "Action Roll" requirement of having a chance of failure, making the dice irrelevant.
- **The Solution:** **Bracket Jumps** (Proposals 1 & 2) provide a more determinate and stable force. They ensure the Quality feels powerful across all levels without breaking the bounds of the 2d10 probability curve or the established resolution table.

---

## Proposal 1: The Single-Bracket Jump (Conservative Shift)
Invoking a Quality ensures the character's baseline performance is always one tier higher.

- **Mechanic:** The final outcome is shifted **one step up** the resolution ladder (e.g., a Fumble becomes a Standard Success).
- **Impact:** Functions as a "floating" +5 bonus that respects the caps of the system.
- **Why it fits:** It is mathematically stable and ensures the Quality is always a relevant, but not game-breaking, factor.

## Proposal 2: The Double-Bracket Jump (Heroic Shift)
The most powerful stable option for high-expertise characters.

- **Mechanic:** The final outcome is shifted **two steps up** the resolution ladder (e.g., a Fumble becomes a Perfect Success; a Disaster becomes a Standard Success).
- **Impact:** Functions as a "floating" +10 bonus.
- **Why it fits:** This is the most determinate force possible. It handles the "more than half" requirement perfectly (10 is 50%+ of all typical attributes) while keeping the math bounded within the 2d10 bell curve.

## Proposal 3: The Expertise Floor (Fixed High Bonus)
The Quality provides a strong, fixed baseline that "carries" the attribute.

- **Mechanic:** Total = 2d10 + Attribute + 12.
- **Impact:** A flat **+12 bonus**.
- **Why it fits:** This bonus is greater than 50% of any Attribute up to 23. This ensures that the Quality is always the dominant factor for the majority of characters, while still being highly significant for legendary masters.

---

## Recommendation for 1-20 Scale
Per the USER's insight, **Proposal 2 (Double-Bracket Jump)** is the superior high-impact option. It provides the dominant mechanical force required without the inflationary risks of doubling raw numbers.
