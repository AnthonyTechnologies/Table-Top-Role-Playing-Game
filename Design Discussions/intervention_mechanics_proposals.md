# Proposal: Intervention Point Mechanics

**Intervention Points (IP)** represent a character's "Authorial Weight"—the ability to nudge fate when neither the environment (Favor) nor their background (Qualities) is enough.

## Principles of Intervention
- **Impact Level**: Should feel more potent than Favor (+2.4 shift) but comparable to The Quality Edge (+5/Bracket shift).
- **Versatility**: Can be used to "patch" a lack of situational or character advantage.
- **Tactical Timing**: Spending **Before** represents preparation/fate; spending **After** represents a last-second salvation.

---

## Proposed Mechanic 1: The Narrative Wildcard (Proxy)
This allows IP to function as a "Universal Key" for existing mechanics.
- **Before the Roll (Favor Proxy)**: Spend 1 IP to gain **Favor** (3d10 keep 2) on any action, regardless of external circumstances.
- **After the Roll (Edge Proxy)**: Spend 1 IP to apply **The Quality Edge** (+1 Bracket Shift) to any action, regardless of whether a Quality Tag applies.

## Proposed Mechanic 2: The Fate Flip (Post-Roll Mitigation)
This is for when the player wants to "undo" a poor result without the risk of Pushing.
- **After the Roll**: Spend 1 IP to **reroll the dice and keep the better result**. 
- *Distinction from Pushing*: Pushing requires a resource cost and you *must* take the new result. The Fate Flip is safer and more narrative.

## Proposed Mechanic 3: The Miracle Shield (Catastrophe Prevention)
- **After the Roll**: Spend 1 IP to ignore a **Catastrophe** (Natural 2) or a **Disaster** bracket. The result is instead treated as a **Fumble** (Success with cost).
- *Narrative Context*: "Fortune favors the bold." A character survives something that should have ended them by sheer narrative plot armor.

## Proposed Mechanic 4: The Heroic Nudge (Pre-Roll Floor)
- **Before the Roll**: Spend 1 IP to establish a **Minimal Result**. Your dice roll cannot sum to less than **11** (the average of 2d10). 
- *Impact*: This mathematically removes the bottom 50% of the bell curve, guaranteeing a higher baseline of success without a "Perfect" auto-win.

---

## Comparison Table

| Mechanic | Impact Level | Timing | Best Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Favor Proxy** | Moderate (~+2.4) | Before | When you lack tactical advantage. |
| **Edge Proxy** | High (+1 Bracket) | After | When you lack expertise but *must* succeed. |
| **Fate Flip** | High (Average shift) | After | When you rolled poorly but don't want to Push. |
| **Miracle Shield** | Save (Situational) | After | Preventing a catastrophic narrative failure. |
| **Heroic Nudge** | Very High (Floor) | Before | Ensuring a "safe" roll for a vital task. |
