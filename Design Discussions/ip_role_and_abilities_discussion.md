# Design Discussion: The Niche of Intervention Points

The user identified two critical design risks:
1. **Mechanical Eclipse**: IP might be redundant if they just copy Favor or Quality Edges.
2. **Complexity Bloat**: High-impact IP uses (like the "Heroic Nudge") might be better as unique character Abilities.

## Refined Proposal: IP as the "Social & Meta" Resource

To prevent IP from being eclipsed, we should move away from raw dice modification and toward **Narrative Control** and **Social Interaction**.

### 1. Migrating Complex Mechanics to "Abilities"
We can strip the "intricate" proposals from universal IP use and move them into the **Character Ability** system.
- **The Heroic Nudge (Floor of 11)**: Becomes a Talent like *Reliable* or *Steady Hand*.
- **The Miracle Shield (Negate Catastrophe)**: Becomes a specialized *Fate-Touched* or *Iron Will* ability.
- *Benefit*: This keeps the core rules simple while making character builds feel unique.

### 2. Defining a Unique Role for IP
If Favor (Environment), Quality Edge (Backstory), and Pushing (Effort) cover the *self*, **Intervention** should cover the **World** and the **Party**.

#### Proposed Universal IP Uses:
- **Intervening for Others**: This is the only mechanic that allows you to help a teammate *after* they roll. Spend 1 IP to grant them a "Helpful Shift" (+1 Bracket) or Favor.
- **Narrative Retcon (The "Lucky Find")**: Spend 1 IP to introduce a minor favorable detail into the scene (e.g., "I spend an IP; there's actually a leaky pipe over the guard's head").
- **The "Safety Net" (Universal Mitigation)**: The *only* way to shift a bracket UP when you **don't** have a relevant Quality Tag. It's the "expensive" version of expertise.

## Summary of the "Modification Hierarchy" (Revised)
- **Favor**: The World (situational dice).
- **The Quality Edge**: The character's Past (expertise shift).
- **Pushing**: The character's Present (resource-draining reroll).
- **Intervention**: The "Meta" / The Party (Fictional inclusion or saving others).
