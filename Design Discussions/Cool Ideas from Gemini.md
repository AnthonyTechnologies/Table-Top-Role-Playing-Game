# Cool Ideas from Gemini

This document tracks the mechanical and narrative ideas proposed by Gemini that have been adopted into the game's core design.

## Core Resolution & Probability
- **2d10 Resolution**: Moving from a flat die **1d20** to **2d10** to create a mathematical bell curve with rounded probabilities. An obvious benefit, its title basically asked me "Why not 2D10?" and I instantly agreed, I did not read any further.
- **"Favor" and "Disfavor"**: Situational modifiers (3d10 keep 2) which works like Advantage/Disadvantage in D&D 5e, but with a more granular probability curve and without the needing to track two separate (2d10) rolls.
- **Status-Based Recovery Rests**: To make rests more meaningful, Gemini proposed adding typed **Status** recovery to rests.
