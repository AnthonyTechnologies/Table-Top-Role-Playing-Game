Abilities
=========

**Abilities** are active specialized techniques, maneuvers, or powers that represent a character's ability to exert their will upon the world. Unlike **Traits**, which are persistent qualities, Abilities require a conscious action or the expenditure of resources (**Stamina**, **Intervention Points**, or **Essence**) to trigger.

For physical play, Abilities are intended to be represented by **Reference Cards**, allowing players to build a custom "hand" of capabilities that reflects their character's current focus and training.

The Anatomy of an Ability
-------------------------

Each ability is defined by several key components, ensuring that every specialized action has clear narrative context and mechanical weight.

1. **Name**
~~~~~~~~~~~
The unique title of the ability (e.g., *Whirlwind Strike*, *Phase Step*, *Greater Healing*).

2. **Category (Power Level)**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The **Category** of an ability represents its complexity, rarity, and overall power level. This aligns with the game's standard skill progression:

*   **Novice:** Common, foundational techniques accessible to most characters.
*   **Adept:** Specialized maneuvers requiring significant training or experience.
*   **Master:** Rare, highly powerful powers representing peak competence.
*   **Grandmaster:** Legendary capabilities that define the absolute limits of mortal potential.

3. **Quality Tags**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Abilities possess their own **Quality Tags**—descriptive keywords that act as tangible qualities of the technique itself (e.g., *[Combat]*, *[Mystic]*, *[Stealth]*). These tags can be invoked during an **Action Roll** via **The Quality Edge**. (See: :ref:`quality_edge`)

4. **Description**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A short narrative explanation of what the ability looks like in the world and how the character performs it.

5. **Activation & Requirements**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Defines exactly **how** and **when** the ability is used. This component includes:

*   **Action Cost:** Whether it requires a standard Action, a Reactive roll, or can be used as a free maneuver.
*   **Resource Cost:** The amount of **Stamina**, **IP**, or other resources required to trigger the effect.
*   **Quality Tag Requirements:** Many abilities require the character to possess specific **Quality Tags** (Identity or Talent) to activate. For example, a "Precision Strike" ability might require the character to have a *[Swordsman]* or *[Duelist]* talent tag.

6. **Effect**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The core mechanical benefit of the ability. This may include direct damage, narrative permissions, bonuses to **Attributes**, or the infliction of a **Status** on a target.

7. **Resolution (Optional)**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Some abilities have a lasting duration rather than an instantaneous effect. This section defines how and when the ability's effect resolves (e.g., *End of Turn*, *End of Scene*, or *Until the character takes damage*).
