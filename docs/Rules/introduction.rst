Introduction: The Core Vision
=============================

Welcome to [Game Name], a tabletop role-playing game system designed to bridge the gap between deep narrative storytelling and tangible mechanical weight. In this system, every choice made at the table—whether it's a daring leap across a chasm or a tense diplomatic negotiation—has a lasting ripple effect on the character and the world.

The Core Design Pillars
-----------------------

This system is built upon four foundational pillars that define its unique approach to play:

1. **Narrative-Mechanical Weight**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
We believe that the outcome of a story beat should persist beyond the immediate roll. Through the **Status-as-Consequence** rule, successes and failures aren't just one-off events; they grant positive or negative **Statuses** that mechanically represent the narrative shift. A *Fumble* might leave you *[Exposed]*, while a *Perfect* success might grant you an *[Inspired]* edge.

2. **Deterministic Strategic Variance**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
By utilizing **2d10** for all Action Rolls, we create a mathematical **Bell Curve**. This ensures that results naturally cluster around the average, making a character's static **Attributes** the primary driver of success. Randomness is a factor, but expertise is the foundation.

3. **Physical Modularity (The Card System)**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The system is designed with physical reference cards in mind. **Traits**, **Techniques**, **Items**, and **Statuses** are all structured as discrete modules. Players can build their character by literal "hand," swapping cards as they progress or suffer consequences, reducing the need for constant character sheet recalculations.

4. **Accessibility (The Low Bar of Entry)**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
While the system supports deep strategy, its core mechanics are designed for quick adoption. By using standardized materials (2d10) and a "Math-Light" design philosophy, players can focus on the fiction and tactical choices rather than complex arithmetic.

The Action Loop
---------------

Gameplay in [Game Name] follows a simple, repeating cycle:

1.  **Intent & Action:** The player describes what their character wants to do.
2.  **The Action Roll:** The player rolls 2d10 and adds their relevant Attribute. (See: :ref:`action_rolls`)
3.  **Persona's Edge:** Players invoke **Persona Tags** from their Identity, Talents, or Items to gain an advantage. (See: :ref:`persona_edge`)
4.  **The Outcome:** The result falls into one of six **Resolution Brackets** (from Disaster to Perfect Success). (See: :ref:`outcome_brackets`)
5.  **Consequence & Evolution:** The narrative moves forward, and the character potentially gains a new **Status** or **Persona Tag**. (See: :ref:`statuses`)

What You Need to Play
---------------------

To start your journey, you will need:

- **2d10 Dice:** For resolving Action Rolls.
- **Character Reference Material:** Either a character sheet or a set of **Trait/Technique Cards**.
- **Reference Cards:** For tracking **Items** and **Statuses**.
- **Colored Poker Chips:** Highly recommended for tracking **Character Resources**. For accessibility, chips could be marked or textured to assist colorblind players.
- **A Game Master (GM):** To facilitate the world and assign outcomes.
- **Fellow Players:** To form a party and explore the story together.
