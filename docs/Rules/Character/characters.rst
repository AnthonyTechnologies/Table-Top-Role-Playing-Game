.. _characters:

Characters
==========

In [Game Name], characters are defined by the intersection of their history, their current focus, and the weight of their destiny. To represent this, a character is composed of several distinct mechanical layers that interact to determine their capabilities and survival.

Character Layers
----------------

Each character is defined by the following eight layers:

1. :ref:`persona`: Narrative descriptors of identity, training, and compulsions.
2. :ref:`attributes`: Raw physical and mental potential that drives Action Rolls.
3. :ref:`traits`: Persistent specialized capabilities and inherent qualities.
4. :ref:`statuses`: Temporary conditions that affect a character's effectiveness.
5. :ref:`techniques`: Specialized maneuvers and powers requiring resource expenditure.
6. :ref:`character_resources`: Vitality pools including Health, Stamina, Essence, and Intervention.
7. :ref:`armor`: External protection and damage reduction against trauma.
8. :ref:`items`: The weaponry, tools, and gear carried by the character.

---

1. Persona
----------
A character's background and personality are defined by **Persona Tags**. These tags represent who the character is, what they know, and the fundamental **Compulsions** that drive them. Persona Tags are the primary way a character's history influences the narrative through **Persona's Edge**.

*   **Identity:** Establishes who a character is (e.g., *Occult Bureau Investigator*, *Disgraced Elven Royal*).
*   **Talents:** Establishes what a character knows or is trained in (e.g., *Master of Clockwork Devices*).
*   **Compulsions:** Establishes fundamental **Imperatives** or **Flaws**—the absolute rules, codes of conduct, or deep-seated urges the character must follow.

(See: :ref:`persona`)

2. Attributes
-------------
**Attributes** represent a character's raw potential across eight categories. They are the primary modifier for all **Action Rolls**, ensuring that a character's inherent competence is the most significant factor in their success.

*   **Strength:** Physical force and lifting capacity.
*   **Endurance:** Resilience and physical stamina.
*   **Agility:** Speed and reflexes.
*   **Dexterity:** Precision and fine motor skills.
*   **Intelligence:** Reasoning and problem-solving.
*   **Willpower:** Mental fortitude and magical potential.
*   **Acuity:** Perception and situational awareness.
*   **Charisma:** Social persuasion and leadership.

(See: :ref:`attributes`)


3. Traits
---------
**Traits** are persistent specialized capabilities representing specialized training, inherent qualities, or long-term growth (e.g., *Fleet-Footed*, *Sharp-Eyed*). They provide consistent mechanical and narrative advantages.

(See: :ref:`traits`)

4. Statuses
-----------
**Statuses** are temporary conditions (e.g., *Stunned*, *Bleeding*, *Inspired*) triggered by damage, environmental effects, or the resolution of actions. They represent the immediate state of the character.

(See: :ref:`statuses`)

5. Techniques
-------------
**Techniques** are active specialized maneuvers or powers (e.g., *Whirlwind Strike*) that require a conscious expenditure of resources. Unlike standard Narrative Actions, Techniques offer consistent mechanical advantages and unique effects.

(See: :ref:`techniques`)

6. Character Resources
----------------------
**Character Resources** are the primary "currencies" of agency in [Game Name]. Rather than relying solely on luck, characters must strategically manage their finite energy to survive, exert effort, and influence the narrative. Each of the four resource pools represents a distinct layer of a character's capacity:

*   **Health:** Physical integrity and survival.
*   **Stamina:** The fuel for physical exertion, muscle fatigue, and tactical maneuvers.
*   **Essence:** The bandwidth for mental focus, spiritual capacity, and supernatural power.
*   **Intervention:** The measure of a character's fate and meta-narrative agency.

Resources are dynamic, constantly fluctuating through **Spending** to trigger immediate effects or **Reserving** to sustain persistent capabilities.

(See: :ref:`character_resources`)

7. Armor
--------
**Armor** provides a static buffer against incoming trauma. It is divided into **Physical Armor** (for kinetic force) and **Ephemeral Armor** (for magical or spiritual effects), reducing the damage taken from successful hits.

(See: :ref:`armor`)

8. Items
--------
**Items** represent the weaponry, tools, and gear carried by the character. This includes **Equipment** assigned to active slots for immediate tactical benefits and **Inventory** contributing to the character's **Encumbrance**.

(See: :ref:`items`)
