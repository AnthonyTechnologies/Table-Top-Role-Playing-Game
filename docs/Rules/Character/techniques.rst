Techniques
==========

**Techniques** are active specialized maneuvers, methods, or powers that represent a character's ability to exert their will upon the world. Unlike **Traits**, which are persistent qualities, Techniques require a conscious action or the expenditure of resources (**Stamina**, **Intervention**, or **Essence**) to trigger.

For physical play, Techniques are intended to be represented by **Reference Cards**, allowing players to build a custom "hand" of capabilities that reflects their character's current focus and training.

The Anatomy of a Technique
--------------------------

Each technique is defined by several key components, ensuring that every specialized action has clear narrative context and mechanical weight.

1. **Name**
~~~~~~~~~~~
The unique title of the technique (e.g., *Whirlwind Strike*, *Phase Step*, *Greater Healing*).

2. **Category (Power Level)**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The **Category** of a technique represents its complexity, rarity, and overall power level. This aligns with the game's standard skill progression:

*   **Novice:** Common, foundational techniques accessible to most characters.
*   **Adept:** Specialized maneuvers requiring significant training or experience.
*   **Master:** Rare, highly powerful powers representing peak competence.
*   **Grandmaster:** Legendary capabilities that define the absolute limits of mortal potential.

3. **Persona Tags**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Techniques possess their own **Persona Tags**—descriptive keywords that act as tangible qualities of the technique itself (e.g., *[Combat]*, *[Mystic]*, *[Stealth]*). These tags can be invoked during an **Action Roll** via **Persona's Edge**. (See: :ref:`persona_edge`)

4. **Description**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
A short narrative explanation of what the technique looks like in the world and how the character performs it.

5. **Activation & Requirements**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Defines exactly **how** and **when** the technique is used. This component includes:

*   **Action Cost:** Whether it requires a standard Action, a Reactive roll, or can be used as a free maneuver.
*   **Resource Cost:** The amount of **Stamina**, **Intervention**, or other resources required to trigger the effect.
*   **Attribute Requirements:** Some techniques require the character to possess a minimum score in a specific **Attribute** (e.g., *Strength 4* or *Intelligence 5*).
*   **Persona Tag Requirements:** Many techniques require the character to possess specific **Persona Tags** (Identity or Talent) to activate. For example, a "Precision Strike" technique might require the character to have a *[Swordsman]* or *[Duelist]* talent tag. **Note:** Any Persona Tag used as a requirement to activate a technique—or any tag of the same type (e.g., a higher tier of the same skill, as determined by the GM)—cannot also be used to gain **Persona's Edge** for that specific action.

6. **Effect**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
The core mechanical benefit of the technique. This may include direct damage, narrative permissions, bonuses to **Attributes**, or the infliction of a **Status** on a target.

7. **Resolution (Optional)**
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Some techniques have a lasting duration rather than an instantaneous effect. This section defines how and when the technique's effect resolves (e.g., *End of Turn*, *End of Scene*, or *Until the character takes damage*).
.. _techniques_methodology:

.. container:: deemphasized

    **Game Methodology: Techniques vs. Narrative Actions**

    In this system, any player can describe their character performing a **Narrative Action** (e.g., "I climb the wall"). These are often resolved through simple **Action Rolls** and may involve a resource expenditure to keep the action economy flowing. **Techniques**, however, are pre-defined mechanical levers that often allow a character to bypass normal rules, target multiple foes, or trigger unique effects like Statuses. While Narrative Actions are flexible and can involve chosen expenditures, Techniques offer specialized mastery and consistent mechanical advantages.
