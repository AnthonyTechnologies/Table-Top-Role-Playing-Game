Attributes
==========

**Attributes** define the character's raw, inherent capabilities. They are the primary driver of success in the 2d10 resolution engine, determining the character's competence in action and the raw power of their Techniques.

*   **Strength:** Physical force and lifting capacity.
*   **Endurance:** Resilience and physical stamina.
*   **Agility:** Speed and reflexes.
*   **Dexterity:** Precision and fine motor skills.
*   **Intelligence:** Reasoning and problem-solving.
*   **Willpower:** Mental fortitude and magical potential.
*   **Acuity:** Perception and situational awareness.
*   **Charisma:** Social persuasion and leadership.

.. container:: deemphasized

    **Game Methodology: The Attribute-Driven Bell Curve**

    By using a 2d10 dice pool, the system ensures that results cluster around the mathematical average. This makes character **Attributes**—which provide a flat, static bonus—extraordinarily meaningful. While a single d20 can produce wild swings of luck, 2d10 allows an expert with a high Attribute to reliably achieve success, reinforcing the idea that a character's inherent training is the most significant factor in their success.

.. container:: deemphasized

    **Game Methodology: Decoupled Effectiveness and Sustainability**

    In this system, **Attributes** are strictly focused on **Effectiveness**—determining how well a character performs an action and the raw power of their maneuvers. Secondary mechanics like **Resource Pools** and **Encumbrance** are intentionally decoupled from attributes to prioritize **Character Customization Freedom**. This prevents the creation of "God Stats" that make a character both exceptionally powerful and impossible to kill, instead shifting sustainability into the **Modular Choice** engine of Traits and Persona Tags.
